Point a client at Holdcall, make one tool call, and it appears here as it is written. The daemon starts on its own the first time a relay needs it.

holdcall init            # shows what it would rewrite
holdcall init --write    # applies it, with backups
holdcall doctor          # every check, with a remedy

Then decide something.

A rule is deny, allow or ask, scoped to a tool and, if you want, one agent and one connector. A held call shows up under Held with its real arguments.

holdcall policy deny delete_repository
holdcall policy ask force_push --connector github
holdcall policy budget 20 --tool list_repos --all-tools
